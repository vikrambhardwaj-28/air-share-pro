How Air Share Pro Works (Architecture)

Zero cloud uploads, 100% device-to-device direct binary pipeline.

01
Acoustic & QR Handshake
Devices generate a secure 6-digit room channel encoded through audible Dual-Tone FSK sound waves or QR optical signals for instant pairing.
02
Direct WebRTC Mesh
A direct encrypted P2P RTCDataChannel opens browser-to-browser. No intermediate file servers or cloud storage relays are used.
03
Zero-Buffer Turbo Stream
Files are sliced into 128KB binary chunks and streamed at raw network speeds. Incoming buffers reconstruct directly in memory for instant auto-download.
