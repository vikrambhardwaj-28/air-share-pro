Zero Database Policy

Air share pro operates entirely without persistent databases. File payloads exist purely in short-lived memory buffers and are purged immediately after the transfer threshold is satisfied.

No IP addresses, telemetry metadata, or user sessions are ever recorded or stored.

Auto-Purge Protocols

AirShare Mode: 120-second lifespan or immediate destruction after 3 batch retrievals.

SoundWave & GravityDrop: Hard ceiling of 60 seconds ephemeral lifespan.
