Zero DB Guarantee

Air share pro eliminates persistent databases. File metadata exists only in temporary RAM structures and is purged instantly after 3 downloads or timeout expirations.

No user IP addresses, identifiers, or transfer logs are preserved.

Auto-Purge Protocols

AirShare Mode: Active for 120 seconds or terminated immediately upon 3 batch downloads.

SoundWave & GravityDrop: Hard ceiling of 60 seconds (1 minute) permanent self-destruct cycle from staging time.
